一、软件准备

1.1 引导镜像freedos11

iso下载地址：http://pingtool.org/downloads/fd11src_live.iso

下载地址：https://docs.broadcom.com/docs-and-downloads/host-bus-adapters/host-bus-adapters-common-files/sas_sata_12g_p16_15_point_release/9311_8i_Pkg_P16_IR_IT_FW_BIOS_for_MSDOS_Windows.zip
